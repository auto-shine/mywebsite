Banbury detailing questions

How much does mobile car detailing cost in Banbury?

An Essential Full Detail starts from £90 and a Deep Clean & Reset from £140. Paint correction starts from £250 and ceramic coating from £550. Medium and large vehicles can cost a little more. Travel is free within 15 miles of Kenilworth, then £1 per mile. We'll confirm the exact charge when you send your postcode.

Do you come to my home in Banbury?

Yes. We detail cars at your home or workplace. A driveway or off-road space is ideal, as we need room to work around the car safely.

Do I need to provide water or power?

No water is needed because we bring our own pure deionised water. Most details need access to a standard 3-pin plug socket.

Can you detail my car if it's parked on the street in Banbury?

Possibly. We need to reach a standard 3-pin plug socket safely, so street parking only works if we can do that from your home or a nearby building. Message us your address and we'll tell you before you book.

What if it rains?

We work under a waterproof gazebo. If the weather would affect a polish or ceramic coating, we'll contact you to rebook.
