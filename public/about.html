About AutoShine

AutoShine is a mobile car detailing service run by Jake, specialising in deep cleaning, paint enhancement and premium exterior detailing across Warwickshire, Worcestershire and, for select bookings, the rest of England & Wales.

The story behind AutoShine

I’m Jake, the face behind AutoShine. I started the business after years of looking after my own cars, always chasing that freshly detailed look – deep gloss, clean trim and a cabin that actually feels nice to sit in.

What began as a hobby quickly turned into word-of-mouth work for friends, family and local customers. Over time, that grew into a full-time mobile detailing service, covering everything from regular deep cleans for family cars to multi-stage machine polishing and ceramic coatings on performance and prestige vehicles.

My approach is simple: treat every car as if it were my own, use products I trust, and be honest about what’s realistic for your paint, your budget and how you actually use the vehicle.

What I do

From daily drivers through to enthusiast and prestige cars, AutoShine is set up to deliver a thorough, careful and honest detailing service at your location.

Local mobile valeting & deep cleaning

Deep interior and exterior cleans for real-world cars – family vehicles, workhorses, daily drivers and everything in between. Ideal if you want the car properly sorted without the hassle of dropping it off somewhere.

  • Essential Clean – regular upkeep
  • Deep Clean Detail – intensive reset
  • Optional add-ons for pet hair, odours and more

Premium exterior detailing & paint correction

For cars where the finish really matters. Single and multi-stage machine polishing, swirl and defect reduction, and ceramic coating options for long-term protection.

  • Premium Exterior package – gloss enhancement
  • Ultimate Exterior package – multi-stage correction
  • Travel and accommodation arranged for national work

Honest advice & aftercare

I’ll always be upfront about what’s achievable, what’s safe for your paint and what I’d personally recommend. You’ll also get simple aftercare guidance so you can keep the car looking better for longer.

  • Realistic expectations, not over-selling
  • Aftercare product suggestions if you want them
  • Support via WhatsApp if you have questions later

Where I work

Day to day, I’m focused on local mobile detailing in Warwickshire and Worcestershire – covering areas such as Kenilworth, Leamington, Warwick, Coventry, Redditch and the surrounding villages.

For Premium and Ultimate exterior packages, I also travel further afield across England & Wales, with travel and accommodation factored into your quote.

  • Local mobile work – Warwickshire & Worcestershire
  • Premium exterior packages – England & Wales
  • Home, workplace or storage locations

How I work

I aim to keep things clear and straightforward. No confusing menus or hidden extras – just a conversation about what you want to achieve and the best way to get there.

  • Clear pricing based on vehicle size, condition and travel
  • Realistic timescales – I won’t rush a job to squeeze another in
  • Communication via WhatsApp, email or phone – whatever suits you

Products & standards

I use a mix of well-regarded detailing brands and techniques built from experience, testing and real-world use on customer cars.

Safe wash methods

Everything starts with safe washing and decontamination – snow foam, pre-wash, two-bucket wash and appropriate brushes and mitts to reduce wash marring as much as possible.

Quality polishes & machines

Dual-action polishers, suitable pad and polish combinations and panel-by-panel inspection to balance improvement with safety. The goal is always to improve your paint, not chase perfection at any cost.

Protection that suits you

From short-term sealants through to multi-year ceramic coatings, I’ll recommend a protection option that matches how long you keep the car, how you wash it and how you actually use it.
