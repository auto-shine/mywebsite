Who is fleet valeting for?

Whether you run a couple of vans or a small fleet of mixed vehicles, regular mobile cleaning helps protect your investment and keeps your business looking professional.

Trades & service vans

Plumbers, electricians, landscapers, builders and more. Clean, well-presented vans help build trust with customers on the driveway or job site.

Company cars & reps

Sales reps and company car drivers represent your brand on the road. Regular exterior and interior care keeps vehicles presentable and pleasant to use.

Small local fleets

Local businesses with a handful of vehicles that need looking after but don’t have an in-house wash bay or time to keep on top of it.

Fleet service options

Services are tailored around how your vehicles are used and how often you’d like them cleaned. Pricing depends on vehicle numbers, sizes, condition and frequency.

One-off deep clean

Ideal if your fleet has been neglected or is going back off lease. A deeper clean to reset things before moving to a lighter maintenance schedule.

  • Thorough exterior wash including wheels & arches
  • Traffic film and bug removal
  • Basic tar & contamination removal on high-impact areas
  • Interior vacuum, wipe down and glass cleaned
  • Tyres dressed for a professional finish

Regular maintenance plan

Scheduled visits to keep vehicles in a consistently presentable condition. Perfect for local fleets that need to look smart without the hassle of organising each clean individually.

  • Monthly, fortnightly or custom schedule
  • Focus on keeping the outside sharp and interiors fresh
  • Flexible to work around shift patterns and vehicle availability

Fleet valeting FAQs

What areas do you cover for fleets?

Fleet work is focused on Warwickshire & Worcestershire and nearby areas. For larger or premium fleets further afield, feel free to get in touch and we can talk options.

Do you need access to water and power?

Ideally, yes. A standard outside tap and plug socket are usually enough. If this isn’t available, let me know when you enquire so I can confirm what’s possible.

Can you clean vehicles during working hours?

In most cases, yes. I’ll work around your schedule and can clean vehicles while staff are on site, so downtime is kept to a minimum.

How is pricing worked out?

Pricing depends on the number of vehicles, their size, how dirty they typically get and how often you’d like them cleaned. Once I know those details, I’ll provide a clear quote.

Want a quote for your fleet?

Send a quick message with how many vehicles you run, your location and how often you’d like them cleaned, and I’ll come back with a straightforward, tailored quote.
