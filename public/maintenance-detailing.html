Maintenance Detailing Scheme

Exclusive, regular upkeep engineered to safely preserve gloss, hydrophobic protection, and cabin clarity after your initial detail.

⚠️ Exclusive Client Maintenance Policy

To protect paintwork from heavy swirl marks and contamination build-up, our Maintenance Detailing Scheme is available exclusively to existing clients whose vehicles have received a Deep Clean Detail, Paint Correction, or Ceramic Coating within the last 6–8 weeks.

Maintenance Price: From £50

What The Maintenance Scheme Covers

A systematic process designed to safely refresh every surface without degrading underlying ceramic or sealant protection.

1. Safe Contact Wash & Wheels

  • pH-Neutral Snow Foam: High-density pre-wash breakdown of surface road film and light grime.
  • Two-Bucket Contact Wash: Hyper-lubricated method using scratch-free plush microfiber mitts.
  • Non-Acidic Wheel Cleaning: Complete structural cleaning of wheel faces, inner barrels, and brake arches.

2. Blow-Dry & Protection Top-Up

  • Touchless Air Drying: Filtered high-pressure air drying to safely clear trapped water from vents and grilles.
  • Gloss & Ceramic Booster: Application of a sacrificial ceramic gloss spray to replenish hydrophobic layers.
  • Tyre & Trim Dressing: Finished with a non-fling satin tyre finish and protected exterior plastics.

3. Interior Refresh & Glass

  • Thorough Vacuum: Deep cabin vacuuming across all floor mats, carpets, boot space, and seats.
  • Cockpit Wipe-Down: Meticulous dusting and satin wipe-down of dashboard, console, and door cards.
  • Streak-Free Glass: Crystal clear cleaning of all interior and exterior glass panels.
