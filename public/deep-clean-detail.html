Deep Clean Detail Service by AutoShine Warks

For the ultimate in vehicle cleanliness and care, our Deep Clean Detail service is designed to tackle even the most stubborn dirt and grime, both inside and out. This comprehensive service goes beyond the standard valet to thoroughly cleanse, decontaminate, and rejuvenate your car, leaving it in pristine condition.

What’s Included:

​Exterior:

  • Hand Wash and Dry: We begin with a meticulous hand wash using premium, pH-balanced products to remove all surface dirt and contaminants. The vehicle is then carefully dried with soft microfiber towels to prevent water spots.

  • Decontamination: We perform a full exterior decontamination process, using specialised products to remove embedded contaminants like road tar, brake dust, and industrial fallout from your paintwork. This step ensures that your car's surface is smooth and ready for further protection.

  • Wheel and Tyre Care: Your wheels and tyres receive an in-depth clean to eliminate brake dust, road grime, and other residues. We finish with a high-quality tyre dressing for a sleek, polished look.

  • Bug and Tar Removal: Tough bug splatters and tar spots are meticulously removed, restoring the smoothness and clarity of your vehicle's exterior.

  • Exterior Windows: All exterior glass surfaces are cleaned to a streak-free finish, enhancing your visibility and the overall appearance of your vehicle.

Interior:

  • Comprehensive Vacuuming: We conduct a thorough vacuuming of the entire interior, including seats, carpets, mats, and the boot, ensuring that no dust, dirt, or debris is left behind.

  • Upholstery and Carpet Shampooing: All fabric surfaces, including seats and carpets, are deeply cleaned using high-quality shampoos to remove stains, dirt, and odours. Leaving your interior looking refreshed and smelling clean.

  • Leather Treatment: If your vehicle has leather seats or interior surfaces, we treat them with specialized leather cleaners and conditioners to restore their softness, shine, and longevity.

  • Interior Surface Cleaning: The dashboard, centre console, door panels, and all plastic and vinyl surfaces are thoroughly cleaned and dressed, leaving them spotless and rejuvenated.

  • Door Jambs: Door jambs are meticulously cleaned, removing any grease or dirt buildup, and ensuring a comprehensive clean throughout your vehicle.

  • Interior Windows: All interior windows are cleaned to a crystal-clear finish, ensuring maximum clarity and an immaculate look.

  • Odour Elimination: We finish by neutralizing any lingering odours, leaving your car with a fresh, inviting scent.

Why Choose Our Deep Clean Detail?

  • Thorough Cleanliness: Our Deep Clean Valet is perfect for vehicles that need extensive cleaning, tackling dirt and grime that standard valets might miss.

  • Decontamination: By removing deeply embedded contaminants, we help preserve your car’s paintwork and prepare it for further protection, such as wax or ceramic coating.

  • Attention to Detail: Every part of your vehicle, inside and out, receives meticulous care, ensuring it looks and feels like new.

  • Convenience: We bring our professional Deep Clean Valet service directly to you, providing exceptional results without any hassle.

Ideal for Restoring Your Vehicle

Our Deep Clean Detail service is ideal for cars that need a little extra attention or for those who want to restore their vehicle to its best possible condition. It’s a comprehensive, deep cleaning solution that leaves your car looking and feeling rejuvenated.
