Frequently Asked Questions

How much does mobile car detailing cost in Warwickshire?

Maintenance schemes start from £50 (exclusively for existing clients), the Essential Full Detail from £90, and the Deep Clean & Reset from £140. Paint correction starts from £250 and ceramic coating packages from £550. Free travel within 15 miles of Kenilworth, then £1 per mile.

Do you need water or electricity at my address?

We bring our own pure deionised water tanks on board, so no water supply is needed from your house. We just require access to a standard 3-pin plug socket.

Which areas do you cover?

We are based in Kenilworth and cover Warwick, Leamington Spa, Stratford-upon-Avon, Southam, Solihull, Redditch, Rugby, and out to Banbury. Drop us a message with your postcode to confirm!

What happens if it rains?

We bring a professional waterproof gazebo setup to protect the vehicle. If weather conditions interfere with ceramic curing or machine polishing, we will contact you promptly to reschedule.
