The duration depends entirely on the specific service level and the base condition of your vehicle. We will provide an estimated timeframe upon booking.
Paint correction is a specialized mechanical polishing process designed to permanently remove surface imperfections from your clear coat—such as swirl marks, light scratches, hazing, and oxidation. This deep refinement restores absolute optical clarity and depth to your paintwork, maximizing gloss and resale value.
Ceramic coatings introduce an incredibly hard, crystalline protective layer over your factory paint. They provide long-lasting chemical and UV protection, preventing paint oxidation and color fading. Their extreme hydrophobic properties repel water, road grime, and harsh environmental fallout, keeping the car exceptionally glossy and making routine washing effortless.
