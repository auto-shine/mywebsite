Monthly Maintenance Plan by AutoShine

Keep your vehicle in top condition all year round with our Monthly Detailing Maintenance Plan. Designed for those who want to preserve the pristine appearance of their car without the hassle, our plan ensures your vehicle receives regular, professional care that keeps it looking its best.

What’s Included:

Regular Exterior Clean:

Each month, we provide a thorough exterior wash using premium, pH-balanced products. This removes dirt, road grime, and environmental contaminants, leaving your vehicle clean and shiny.

Sealant Refresh:

To maintain the protective barrier on your vehicle’s paint, we apply a fresh layer of our exclusive sealant, ensuring long-lasting protection and a glossy finish. This helps to preserve the work done during your initial detailing service.

Wheel and Tyre Care:

Your wheels and tyres receive regular attention, including cleaning and dressing, to keep them free of brake dust and grime. This enhances the overall appearance of your vehicle and ensures a polished look.

Interior Refresh:

We take care of your car’s interior with a quick vacuum and wipe-down of all surfaces, ensuring it stays clean and inviting. This service helps to prevent the buildup of dust, dirt, and debris between more in-depth cleanings.

Glass Cleaning:

All windows and mirrors are cleaned to a streak-free finish, improving visibility and enhancing the clarity of your vehicle's glass surfaces.

Why Choose Our Monthly Detailing Maintenance Plan?

  • Consistent Care: Regular maintenance helps to preserve the appearance and condition of your vehicle, preventing the buildup of dirt and grime that can damage your paintwork over time.

  • Convenience: Our mobile service comes to you, making it easy to keep your car looking great without disrupting your schedule.

  • Cost Savings: By opting into our Monthly Maintenance Plan, you benefit from discounted rates compared to booking individual services, making it an economical choice for ongoing car care.

  • Peace of Mind: With our professional team taking care of your vehicle regularly, you can enjoy the peace of mind that comes from knowing your car is always in top condition.

Perfect for Busy Car Owners

Our Monthly Maintenance Plan is ideal for those who want to keep their car looking immaculate but don’t have the time to manage regular cleanings themselves. It’s the perfect way to ensure your vehicle always makes a great impression.
